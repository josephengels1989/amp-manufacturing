About Ampersand

Custom Magnetic Components, Built in Berkeley

Ampersand Manufacturing designs and produces custom inductors, transformers, and magnetic assemblies for production applications. We work with ferrite, silicon-iron, and 1J85 permalloy cores, wind with 18 to 34 AWG copper and nickel wire, and deliver every order RoHS compliant and lead-free.

Our Story

Founded on a Simple Idea

Joe Engels and Khuyen Nguyen started Ampersand Manufacturing in Berkeley, California, with a clear goal: build custom magnetic components to exact specifications, test every unit, and respond to every inquiry within 24 hours.

The company's first client was NK Technologies in San Jose, a manufacturer of current sensors for industrial power monitoring. That relationship began in April 2025 with a single product line. Within a year, it grew to six active product lines and more than 150,000 cores delivered.

Today, Ampersand supplies toroidal inductors, bobbin-wound coils, filter chokes, power transformers, and custom magnetic assemblies to companies in power electronics, industrial controls, current sensing, and medical devices.
