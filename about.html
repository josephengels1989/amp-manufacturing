What We Do

Custom inductors and magnetic components for precision manufacturing

We design and manufacture toroidal coils, bobbin-wound inductors, filter chokes, power transformers, and custom magnetic assemblies. We work with ferrite and silicon-iron cores, wire gauges from 18 AWG to 34 AWG, and specifications ranging from single prototypes to production runs of thousands of units.

Our production facility in Ho Chi Minh City operates with a team of skilled winding technicians and engineers. We manage quality control at every stage. We review test data personally on every batch. We maintain real-time visibility of your production run. We respond to your specifications, not the other way around.

We supply companies in power electronics, medical devices, industrial controls, and sensing applications. We meet RoHS compliance standards and work to your exact specifications. We deliver samples quickly. We quote fair pricing. We stand behind every unit we produce.
