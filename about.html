About Us

We're not here just to sell an inductor.

The inductor is what we make. But what we actually deliver is the feeling that someone has your back. That your order matters, your timeline matters, and the person on the other end of the phone actually cares whether it works.

Our Story

We built this because the way things worked wasn't working.

Before Ampersand, we spent years watching the same thing happen. Companies that needed custom inductors, whether a few thousand a month or a full production contract, kept running into the same walls. Overseas suppliers with tariff uncertainty and emails that vanish. Domestic shops that over-promise and under-deliver. Nobody built to be a real partner through the messy middle of actual production.

We didn't think people should have to settle for that. Whether you're a small assembly shop placing your first custom order or a contract manufacturer looking to add toroidal winding to your capabilities, you deserve someone who actually shows up. Not someone who takes the order and disappears. Someone who answers the phone, thinks alongside you, and treats your project like it matters. Because it does.

So we built Ampersand. Not to be the biggest. To be the one you trust.

What we believe:

The way you treat any order tells people everything about who you are. We treat every one like it's the only one.

Most problems in manufacturing start with silence. We communicate early, clearly, and often. Even when the news isn't what you want to hear.

These components go into systems people depend on. Medical devices. Industrial controls. Safety equipment. Cutting corners isn't an option when lives are downstream.

Doing the right thing and doing the profitable thing are the same thing, if you're patient enough to let it play out.

The People

Who's behind Ampersand

Ampersand was founded by a university professor and a psychotherapist, both out of Berkeley, California. How we ended up in the inductor business is, honestly, a story of sheer chance. But what kept us here was not.

What we knew about was some of the hardware beneath the magic of modern technology. One component in particular: the inductor. A humble, physical part. Nothing more than a ring of steel or ferrite wrapped tightly in thin copper wire. Inductors are the tiny beating hearts that power the modern world. We fell in love with the work. Selecting between ferrite and powdered iron for a specific frequency range. Reviewing winding resistance and saturation current on a custom run. Managing traceability and documentation for UL and RoHS compliance. The deeper we went, the more we realized this was exactly the kind of challenge we'd been looking for.

And we brought ourselves into it. We listen first. We ask questions before we quote. We design solutions around what you actually need, not what's easiest to produce. We personally oversee every detail because we believe that's the only way to guarantee what ships is what was promised. A professor and a therapist probably weren't supposed to end up here. But it turns out that a career spent caring about people is pretty good preparation for running a company that does the same.

Why This Work Matters
