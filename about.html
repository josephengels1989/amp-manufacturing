About Ampersand

We source the cores, wind the inductors, and deliver the finished product.

Custom toroidal inductors and transformers for power electronics, industrial controls, medical devices, and safety-critical systems. Non-standard dimensions, tight tolerances, and the kind of mixed-volume orders that most shops won't touch.

Why We Exist

The inductor market has a gap. We fill it.

If you need a million standard inductors, there are factories overseas that will do it cheap. If you need a custom toroidal built to your exact specification — a few hundred a month, or a few thousand, with full documentation and someone who actually answers when you call — your options narrow fast.

We built Ampersand to be that option. U.S.-owned, Berkeley-based, with the technical range to handle ferrite, powdered iron, amorphous, nanocrystalline, MPP, and sendust cores. Wire gauges from 2 AWG to 46 AWG. Litz, rectangular, foil, triple-insulated. Sub-microhenry to 100+ millihenry. Operating range from −55°C to +155°C. RoHS compliant, UL/CSA available. Every unit tested before it ships.

We're not the biggest. We're the manufacturer that treats your 500-piece order with the same rigor as a 50,000-piece production run.

What we stand on:

Every unit tested. Every shipment documented. No spot checks. No sampling.

If something changes — materials, timeline, anything — you hear about it from us first. Not after the fact.

These components go into medical devices, industrial controls, and safety-critical systems. The margin for error is zero.

The companies that last are the ones that earn trust. Not the ones that chase volume.

The People

Who runs Ampersand

Ampersand was founded by a university professor and a psychotherapist — both out of Berkeley, California. An unusual background for an inductor company. But what drew us in was the precision of the work: selecting between ferrite and powdered iron for a specific frequency range. Reviewing winding resistance and saturation current on a custom run. Managing traceability and documentation for UL and RoHS compliance.

The deeper we went, the more we realized this was exactly the kind of work we were built for. Detail-oriented. Systematic. Technical enough to stay interesting and consequential enough to stay serious.

We brought a different perspective to manufacturing. We listen before we quote. We ask about the application, not just the spec. And we personally oversee production because that's the only way to guarantee that what ships is what was promised.

Joe Engels & Khuyen Nguyen
Co-Founders & Co-CEOs

The Standard

We test every batch. We document every shipment. We maintain full traceability from core material to finished product. Not because a checklist says to — because the application demands it.

That's the discipline we bring to every order, regardless of size.
