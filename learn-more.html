How We Work

Built for accountability, not volume.

We run a tight operation. Here's what that looks like in practice — from how we communicate to how we verify quality.

01

Direct Communication

You'll have a direct line to the people managing your order. Not a ticketing system. Not a sales rep who hands you off to someone else. When you call, we answer. When something changes, we tell you.

Most problems in manufacturing start with silence — a delayed shipment nobody mentioned, a material substitution nobody approved. We communicate early and clearly, especially when the news isn't what you want to hear.

02

U.S.-Based, Globally Sourced

Berkeley, California. U.S.-owned. We source materials globally through vetted suppliers and manage the logistics — tariffs, lead times, customs, quality control — so you deal with one company and one standard.

If your application requires a specific core material from a specific manufacturer, we source it. If you have a preferred supplier, we coordinate with them directly. The complexity of international supply chains is our problem, not yours.

03

Verified Quality

100% tested before shipment. Inductance, DCR, hi-pot — verified against your specification, not sampled. Every batch ships with full documentation: test reports, certificates of conformance, traceability records.

These components go into medical devices, industrial controls, and safety-critical systems. We built our quality process around that reality, not around what's convenient for production.

04

Operational Discipline

We run a tight operation. Your specs stay on file. Reorders are seamless. If something goes wrong, we fix it and tell you what we changed to prevent it from happening again.

The way you treat a 200-piece order tells people everything about how you'll treat a 20,000-piece order. We hold the same standard regardless of volume, because that's the only standard worth holding.

We didn't build Ampersand to be the biggest. We built it to be the one you don't have to worry about.

Send us your drawings. We'll review the specs, flag any issues, and give you a straight answer within one business day.
