Quality Process

Four Stages of Inspection

Every component passes through four quality checkpoints before it ships. Each stage has documented pass/fail criteria specific to the product line and customer specification.

1

Incoming Material Inspection

Cores, wire, tape, and solder are inspected on receipt. Core dimensions (OD, ID, height) are verified against the specification. Wire gauge and insulation class are confirmed. Material certifications from suppliers are reviewed and filed for traceability.

Verified: dimensions, material certs, visual condition
2

In-Process Testing

During winding, operators verify turn count, winding distribution, and lead length at regular intervals. DCR spot-checks are performed throughout the run to catch drift before it becomes a batch-level issue. Any unit outside tolerance is flagged and quarantined.

Checked: turn count, DCR spot-check, visual
3

Final Inspection

Every finished unit is tested individually. DCR is measured and recorded. Lead lengths are verified. Visual inspection confirms proper winding distribution, tape application, solder quality, and absence of nicks or damage to the wire insulation. Units that fail any test are rejected.

Tested: 100% DCR, visual, dimensional
4

Packaging and Documentation

Approved units are packaged for shipment with protective materials appropriate to the component. Every lot ships with a complete test report (individual DCR readings), certificate of conformance, and material traceability documentation.

Included: test report, CoC, traceability records

Testing

What We Test and How

Every unit produced at Ampersand undergoes individual testing before it leaves the production floor. Here are the core measurements we perform on every component.

  • DC Resistance (DCR). Measured on every unit with a precision LCR meter. Results recorded and included in the lot test report. Tolerances from ±5% to ±15% depending on product specification.
  • Visual Inspection. Winding uniformity, tape application, lead tinning quality, wire insulation integrity, and solder joint appearance. Every unit inspected by a trained operator.
  • Dimensional Verification. Lead length, overall OD after winding, minimum ID after winding, and core height. Measured against drawing specifications with calibrated tools.
  • Solder Quality. SN100 lead-free solder application inspected on tinned leads. Tin length verified per spec (typically 1/8" to 1/4" depending on the product line).
  • Winding Distribution. Turn count verified. Even distribution around the core confirmed. Lap count and winding pattern checked against the product drawing.
