How We Work

Conversation to doorstep.

First production in about two months, repeat orders in as little as two weeks.

1. Initial Conversation

Tell us what you need: requirements, quantities, timeline, application. We'll scope the job and confirm what we can deliver.

2. Drawing Review

Send us your drawings, schematics, or reference documentation. We review for manufacturability, flag clearance problems, thermal concerns, or material availability, and come back with specific technical questions.

3. Quote & Timeline

Initial quote within 24 hours. Line-item pricing, fully inclusive, with lead times for every line.

4. Material Sourcing

We source cores, wire, and insulation materials through established suppliers. If your application requires a specific core manufacturer or wire type, we coordinate with them directly.

5. Production

Winding, assembly, and in-process verification. First orders typically take about two months from samples to delivery. Repeat orders run as fast as two weeks. You'll get progress updates at each milestone: material receipt, winding completion, testing, and ship date confirmation.

6. Testing & Verification

Every unit tested for inductance, DCR, and hi-pot. If your application requires saturation current, leakage inductance, or core loss verification, we test those too. Full test data ships with every batch.

7. Delivery

Shipped with full documentation: test reports, certificates of conformance, traceability records. Your drawings stay on file. Reorders are seamless.
