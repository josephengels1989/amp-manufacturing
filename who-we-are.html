Who We Are

Ampersand creates magnetics that perform and last under pressure.

What we do is pay attention to the details that tend to disappear between schematic intent and shop-floor reality. How tolerances stack, where heat actually concentrates, which steps are operator-sensitive, and which parameters must be controlled rather than inspected. This kind of attention is quiet by nature. But it determines whether a part behaves the same way on its thousandth build as it did on its first.

We've learned that our special sauce, what we call discipline, protects schedules later, when changes are expensive and options narrow. We are selective about what we take on. Not every magnetic design is ready for production. When requirements are incomplete or assumptions remain unresolved, we slow the process down long enough to make the risks visible.

Variation is treated as an expected condition. Designs and processes are built with that variation in mind, so normal differences in materials or handling do not push performance outside acceptable bounds. When behavior shifts, we detect it early, trace the cause, and correct it at the source. We incorporate high-order knowledge of practical instability for the purpose of eliminating it.

An important point: As a guiding principle we do not separate engineering from manufacturing. Decisions made upstream are expected to survive contact with specific use cases. That expectation shapes how we review drawings and map the arc of a project. By the time one of our projects hits the floor, every open question about the part has been closed.

Our capabilities include:

  • Custom magnetic components and assemblies built to customer drawings and electrical requirements.
  • Controlled manufacturing processes with defined inspection points and documented quality expectations.
  • Speedy, blunt, practical communication from initial review through production release and delivery.
  • Tight, trustable lead times.
  • Flexible order quantities supporting both engineering builds and volume programs.
