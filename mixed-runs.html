03 — Mixed Runs

Multiple part numbers. One purchase order. Zero confusion.

Variable SKUs, split quantities, mid-order spec changes. Other manufacturers call this a headache. We built our operation for it.

The reality

Your BOM isn't one line item. Why should your inductor order be?

Real production is messy. A single product might need three different inductors across two board revisions. A product family might share a form factor but require different inductance values, current ratings, or winding configurations.

Most manufacturers want clean, simple, single-SKU orders. We built our operation for the opposite — because that's what our customers actually need.

Real-world scenarios

The kinds of orders we handle every week

Multi-SKU

8 part numbers, one purchase order

Different inductance values, different current ratings, same core family. One shipment, one point of contact, zero confusion.

Split quantities

500 of this, 50 of that, 2,000 of the other

Variable quantities across the order. We don't force you into uniform batch sizes. You order what you need.

Rev changes

Updated specs on parts 3 and 7, mid-order

Engineering changes happen. We adapt without starting over, without drama, without punitive change fees.

Product families

Same platform, six power variants

A family of products sharing mechanical specs but requiring different electrical characteristics. We manage the matrix.

Prototypes + production

25 samples of the new design, 1,000 of the current one

Development and production running in parallel. Different specs, different quantities, same order, same timeline.

Custom packaging

Each SKU labeled, separated, and documented

Mixed doesn't mean mixed up. Every part number arrives clearly identified with its own test data and traceability records.

Our process

How we keep complexity from becoming your problem

01

Single intake, complete picture

Send us everything — all SKUs, all specs, all quantities. We build a master production plan that accounts for dependencies, shared materials, and optimal sequencing.

02

Intelligent scheduling

We sequence production to minimize changeover time and maximize material efficiency. Parts that share cores or wire gauges run together. Your order moves faster because we plan smarter.

03

Per-SKU quality verification

Each part number is tested independently against its own specification. No batch assumptions. No shortcuts because "it's basically the same part." It's not — and we know that.

04

Organized delivery

Every SKU arrives labeled, separated, and accompanied by its own documentation. Your receiving team knows exactly what's in every box without opening it.

The mindset

Complexity is a service, not a surcharge.

Some manufacturers charge a premium for mixed orders because they see them as a nuisance — extra setup, extra paperwork, extra risk. We see it differently. Mixed runs are where operational discipline matters most. They're the orders that separate a capable manufacturer from one that just fills simple POs.

The harder your order is to get right, the more our process matters.
