Power Electronics

Inductors and Transformers for Power Conversion

Power electronics designers need magnetic components that handle the electrical stresses of continuous operation: ripple current, core saturation margins, thermal limits, and efficiency targets. We manufacture the inductors and transformers that go into AC/DC converters, DC/DC converters, switching power supplies, and linear power supplies.

For power-frequency applications (50/60 Hz), we use grain-oriented 3% silicon-iron cores with saturation flux density of 1.6 to 2.0 Tesla. For switching applications above 20 kHz, we use ferrite cores selected for low core loss at your operating frequency. Toroidal geometry keeps radiated EMI low and efficiency high.

Typical Products

  • Filter chokes for AC line and output filtering
  • Power inductors for buck, boost, and flyback converters
  • Power transformers for voltage conversion
  • Common mode chokes for conducted EMI suppression
  • Energy storage inductors for resonant converters

Core Materials

Silicon-Iron, Ferrite

Wire Gauges

18 to 34 AWG

Current Sensing & Instrumentation

Precision Measurement Components

Current sensing applications demand the highest permeability cores and the most consistent winding. A current transformer (CT) measures current by sensing the magnetic field it creates. The core material determines sensitivity. The winding determines accuracy. Both must be precisely controlled.

We manufacture CTs on 1J85 permalloy cores with permeability exceeding 100,000. These cores detect small currents accurately across wide dynamic ranges. Our winding team distributes turns evenly across the full 360 degrees of the toroid for linear output and consistent coupling.

This is the work we know best. We supply NK Technologies in San Jose with current sensing toroids for their industrial power monitoring product line. Over 150,000 cores delivered. Zero rejected lots.

Typical Products

  • Current transformers for AC current measurement
  • Split-core and solid-core CT assemblies
  • High-accuracy metering-grade current transformers
  • Zero-sequence CTs for ground-fault detection
  • Rogowski coil cores for transient measurement

Core Material

1J85 Permalloy

Permeability

100,000+

Industrial Controls

Built for Demanding Industrial Environments

Industrial control systems operate in environments with electrical noise, temperature extremes, vibration, and continuous duty cycles. The magnetic components inside these systems must perform reliably under these conditions year after year.

We build inductors and transformers for motor drives, programmable logic controllers (PLCs), automation equipment, and power distribution systems. Silicon-iron cores handle the current levels and flux densities that industrial power circuits demand. Toroidal geometry minimizes radiated noise that could interfere with sensitive control signals nearby.

Typical Products

  • EMI chokes for motor drive output stages
  • Power transformers for control panel power distribution
  • Current sensing CTs for motor overload protection

Telecommunications

EMI Filtering and Signal Integrity

Telecom infrastructure requires magnetic components that deliver consistent electrical performance across long production runs. EMI filter inductors suppress conducted emissions that would degrade signal quality. Signal transformers provide impedance matching and galvanic isolation in communication circuits.

We use ferrite cores selected for stable permeability and impedance at telecom operating frequencies. Toroidal construction keeps radiated emissions low, critical in equipment racks where dozens of systems operate in close proximity. Consistent winding tension and turn count ensure repeatable performance from unit to unit.

Typical Products

  • Common mode chokes for EMI suppression
  • Signal transformers for impedance matching
  • Filter inductors for power-over-ethernet (PoE) circuits
  • Isolation transformers for telecom power supplies
  • EMI filters for base station and repeater equipment
