What Helps

The more context, the better

Drawings & Specs

Electrical schematic, mechanical drawing, or specification sheet. CAD files, PDFs, or whatever you have. We work with what is available.

Volume & Schedule

How many units per month? Is demand steady or variable? When do you need first articles? When does production need to start?

Application Context

What system does this go into? What operating environment? Knowing the application helps us evaluate feasibility and flag potential issues.
