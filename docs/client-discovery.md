## Who They Serve

### Ideal Customer
> "Our ideal customer is a small to medium sized assembly or manufacturing company, often based in California or the U.S., that needs toroidal inductors but does not want to maintain an in-house winding operation. They value strong customer service, clear communication, and partnership over transactional buying."

> "They are not looking for anonymous mass production. They are looking for people behind the process, a partner who understands that the work needs to be done well, not just done."

### The Problem ANT Solves
> "The core problem we solve is the gap between prototype-scale work and mass production. When a customer needs a few thousand inductors per month, especially with custom specs or changing requirements, most winding shops either cannot accommodate the volume or are not interested."

> "Many of our customers have previously outsourced winding overseas and are frustrated. Language barriers, time zone delays, tariffs, uncertainty around quality, and lack of accountability create constant friction."

### How Customers Find Them
> "Primarily through word of mouth and referrals. We build strong relationships with existing clients, and those clients talk about us. Prospective customers hear about us through industry connections or direct outreach, then visit our website to validate what they have heard."

> "Our reputation and relationships lead the way. The website reinforces that trust once they arrive."

## What Sets ANT Apart

### Trust and Flexibility (Updated response)
> "What truly sets us apart is trust and flexibility. We specialize in small to mid-size production runs, mixed batches, and non-standard dimensions and specifications. Many winding shops are set up for very high volume and are not interested in runs like 1,000 to 2,000 inductors per month. That leaves a real gap for companies that need reliable production without committing to massive quantities."

> "We operate as a partner who can handle complexity, variability, and change. Customers trust us because we communicate clearly, respond quickly, and take ownership of the work. They know their project will not be treated as an inconvenience or pushed aside for larger orders."

### Values (not the pitch, but how they operate)
> "An important distinction is that we are not trying to capture customers through 'we're a green company and ethical'. It's what we do, but it's not what we're pitching or emphasizing. It's just what me and Quinn believe, so it's how we run the company."

> "We genuinely care about people, our employees and our clients. We believe this work matters. These components go into safety equipment, sensors, and systems people rely on every day."

## What They're Selling

> "Primarily, we sell a service. The product, toroidal inductors, is the output, but the value is in how we deliver it. Anyone can sell a part. What we provide is clean communication, reliable timelines, agility, and people-centered technical support."

> "We do more than wind inductors: this is key! There's quite a bit to it. We review drawings, source materials on their behalf, test products, and think alongside them."
