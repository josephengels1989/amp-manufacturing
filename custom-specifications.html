01 / Custom Specifications

Your drawings. Your dimensions. Your part.

Every inductor and transformer we build starts with your specifications and stays faithful to them through delivery.

The reality

We shape our process around your specifications.

Standard catalogs work when your application is standard. The engineers we work with are building power systems, medical equipment, defense hardware. Products where the inductor is integral.

When your application calls for a non-standard core size, a specific winding pattern, or tolerances tighter than what's on the shelf, that's the work we're here for.

Capabilities

What we build to

Non-Standard Dimensions

OD, ID, height. If it's on your drawing, we match it. Exact dimensions, exact form factor.

Winding Configurations

Single-layer, multi-layer, sectored, bifilar, progressive. We wind to your schematic, exactly as drawn.

Core Materials

Ferrite, iron powder, amorphous, nanocrystalline, MPP, sendust. We source what your application demands.

Tight Tolerances

Inductance, DCR, leakage, saturation current. We test and verify every parameter you specify. Every unit.

Insulation & Compliance

UL, CE, RoHS, hi-pot requirements. We build to your full regulatory and electrical environment.

Termination & Mounting

Through-hole leads, flying leads, header pins, surface mount bases. Whatever your PCB layout or assembly process requires.

From drawing to doorstep

How a custom order moves

Day 1

You send us your drawings

Specifications, schematics, target parameters. Whatever you have. We review the same day.

Days 1–2

Engineering review & quote

We assess manufacturability, flag any questions, and return a detailed quote within days. Line-item pricing, clear timelines.

Days 3–7

Prototype production

First articles built to your exact requirements. Tested, measured, documented. You approve before we scale.

Week 2–4

Full production run

Every unit wound, tested, and inspected against your requirements. Regular updates throughout. We send updates before you have to ask.

Delivery

Shipped with full documentation

Test reports, certificates of conformance, traceability records. The parts arrive ready for your production floor.

If it's on your drawing, we'll build it. If it's not possible, we'll tell you why, and what we'd do instead.
