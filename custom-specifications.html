01 — Custom Specifications

Your drawings. Your dimensions. Your part.

We don't sell from a catalog. Every inductor and transformer we build starts with your specifications — and stays faithful to them through delivery.

The reality

Most manufacturers want you to fit their process. We fit ours to you.

Standard catalogs work when your application is standard. But the engineers we work with aren't building standard things. They're building power systems, medical equipment, defense hardware — products where the inductor isn't interchangeable, it's integral.

When your specs call for a non-standard core size, a specific winding pattern, or tolerances tighter than what's on the shelf — that's not a problem for us. That's the work.

Capabilities

What we build to

Non-Standard Dimensions

OD, ID, height — if it's on your drawing, we match it. No rounding to the nearest catalog size. No compromises on form factor.

Winding Configurations

Single-layer, multi-layer, sectored, bifilar, progressive. We wind to your schematic, not to our convenience.

Core Materials

Ferrite, iron powder, amorphous, nanocrystalline, MPP, sendust. We source what your application demands.

Tight Tolerances

Inductance, DCR, leakage, saturation current — we test and verify every parameter you specify. Not spot checks. Every unit.

Insulation & Compliance

UL, CE, RoHS, hi-pot requirements. We build to your regulatory environment, not just your electrical requirements.

Termination & Mounting

Through-hole leads, flying leads, header pins, surface mount bases. Whatever your PCB layout or assembly process requires.

From drawing to doorstep

How a custom order moves

Day 1

You send us your drawings

Specifications, schematics, target parameters — whatever you have. We review the same day.

Days 1–2

Engineering review & quote

We assess manufacturability, flag any questions, and return a detailed quote. No weeks of silence. No generic pricing.

Days 3–7

Prototype production

First articles built to your exact spec. Tested, measured, documented. You approve before we scale.

Week 2–4

Full production run

Every unit wound, tested, and inspected against your requirements. Regular updates throughout — you'll never have to ask where things stand.

Delivery

Shipped with full documentation

Test reports, certificates of conformance, traceability records. The parts arrive ready for your production floor.

If it's on your drawing, we'll build it. If it's not possible, we'll tell you why — and what we'd do instead.
