Materials

Core Materials and Wire Types

The right core material and wire selection determine how your component performs under load. We match materials to your application requirements.

◈

Ferrite Cores

High-frequency switching applications. Permeability from 750 to 15,000. Available in toroidal geometries from 0.878" to 1.424" ID. Ideal for EMI suppression, common mode chokes, and high-frequency power conversion.

◆

Silicon-Iron Cores

Grain-oriented 3% silicon-iron for power-frequency applications. Saturation flux density of 1.6 to 2.0 Tesla. Tape wound toroids in 7 mil and 9 mil strip thicknesses. Epoxy and powder coat finishes available.

◉

1J85 Permalloy Cores

High-permeability nickel-iron alloy for current sensing applications. Permeability exceeding 100,000. Precision epoxy coated. The core material behind accurate current measurement across industrial power monitoring systems.

◌

Copper Magnet Wire

Available in 18, 26, and 34 AWG. Single-coat enamel insulation rated to 155°C per NEMA MW80-C. 18 AWG for high-current bobbin windings. 34 AWG for high-turn-count toroidal coils.

◍

Nickel Wire

34 AWG nickel magnet wire for specialized sensing and instrumentation applications. Used in high-permeability toroidal assemblies where copper wire characteristics affect measurement accuracy.

▤

Tape and Coatings

Yellow Mylar insulation tape (3M Type 74, 1/2" width). Epoxy core coatings at 0.015" thickness. Powder coat finishes for tape-wound toroids. SN100 lead-free solder for all lead tinning.
